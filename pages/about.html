My story

Just getting started — and keeping a record of it.

Hi, I'm Sa Em Sromem, a first-year IT and Web Programming student from Cambodia. This site is both my portfolio and my logbook: every page tracks a stage of what I actually know right now, not where I'd like to pretend I am.

I enjoy figuring out how websites are put together, breaking things on purpose to see how they fail, and rebuilding them a bit cleaner each time. Two small projects live on the Projects page so far — more are in progress.

I don't think good developers are made by knowing everything up front. They're made by staying curious, debugging patiently, and showing up again the next day.

Learn it. Build it. Break it. Fix it. Repeat.
